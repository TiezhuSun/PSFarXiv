# Limitations and Non-Claims

## Required limitations

1. **No human expert validation.** Agreement between GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 5 is evidence about two model-based coding systems under the PSF manual; it is not proof of expert-human validity.
2. **Shared discourse/training overlap is possible.** Frontier models may share safety literature, annotation conventions, or correlated biases.
3. **Purposive 30-case corpus.** The sample is deliberately stratified and cannot estimate real-world failure prevalence.
4. **B coverage gap.** Situated Recognition is not positively exercised by the present corpus.
5. **Rare-code uncertainty.** Per-code F1 for C/G and especially any absent category must not be overinterpreted.
6. **G construct ambiguity.** Standalone Outcome & Feedback criteria are not fully shared across coder systems.
7. **X auxiliary instability.** The External Control threshold is broader and less stable than Y.
8. **Model-native confidence is not comparable.** Confidence labels show substantial model-specific calibration differences.
9. **Mitigation differentiation is code-conditioned.** It is exploratory actionability evidence, not an independent causal intervention-selection test.
10. **Evidence packet compression.** Blind case packets summarize primary sources; they do not reproduce every experimental detail from the underlying papers.
11. **Recovery ceiling changes.** Although technical recovery was rule-based and source-preserving, the final usable output for 26 observations was generated under a larger output ceiling than the original call.
12. **Framework authorship / coder design.** The ontology and corpus were designed by the research team; future confirmatory work should separate construct design, case selection, and human coding roles more strongly.

## Prohibited novelty claims

Do not claim:
- “existing taxonomies are flat”;
- “AI safety has not been treated as a process”;
- “PSF invents specification, monitoring, multi-agent, or adversarial risk”;
- “慎独 discovers observed/unobserved behavior differences”;
- “知行合一 discovers the knowledge–behavior gap”;
- “知止 is the first formalization of knowing when not to act”;
- “Chinese philosophy predicts modern AI safety.”

## Correct novelty framing

PSF's potential contribution is the **unit of diagnosis**: locating the earliest evidence-supported transition at which an intended/authorized objective ceases to produce safe situated behavior, while keeping external control and adversarial context cross-cutting.

The empirical contribution is that this transition-based decomposition can be operationalized with nontrivial reproducibility and produces localized falsification targets.
