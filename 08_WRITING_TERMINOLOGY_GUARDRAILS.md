# Writing and Terminology Guardrails

## Required wording

Prefer:
- “demonstrated recognition”
- “elicited safety judgment”
- “expressed safety judgment”
- “observable behavior / trajectory”
- “operative objective/role representation”
- “perceived oversight/evaluation status”
- “technical missingness”
- “replicate runs”
- “model-based coder systems”
- “initial evidence”
- “pilot”
- “stratified purposive corpus”

Avoid unless operationally justified:
- “the model knew”
- “the model believed”
- “the model intended”
- “true goal”
- “inner preference”
- “independent coders” for six model runs
- “ground truth label”
- “prevalence”

## Philosophy wording

Use:
- “inspired by”
- “philosophical resource”
- “conceptual scaffold”
- “motivates the distinction”

Avoid:
- “equivalent to”
- “predicted”
- “anticipated modern AI safety”
- “ancient solution to AI”

## Statistical wording

Use:
- “exact agreement”
- “Cohen’s κ”
- “Krippendorff’s α”
- “descriptive pooled pairwise”
- “case-level modal consensus”

Do not imply pooled run-pairs are independent observations.

## U wording

U = Unknown / Insufficient Evidence.

Never paraphrase U as:
- “no failure,”
- “safe,”
- “other,”
- “coder abstention because uncertain” without specifying insufficient evidence for the core transition.
